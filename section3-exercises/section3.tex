\section*{Pop Quiz (2D sharding across 1 axis)}

$A = \text{fp32}[1024, 4096]$, \quad sharding: $A[I_{XY}, J]$,\quad $\text{mesh} = \{X{:}8,\, Y{:}2\} \Rightarrow$ total 16 devices.

Each local shape: $\text{fp32}\left[\tfrac{1024}{16},\, 4096\right] = \text{fp32}[64, 4096]$.

Local shard is $4 \cdot 64 \cdot 4096$ bytes.

On H100 (bw $= 3.4\mathrm{e}12$ bytes/s):
\[
T_{\text{load}} = \frac{4 \cdot 64 \cdot 4096}{3.4\mathrm{e}12}\,\text{s} = 3 \times 10^{-7}\,\text{s}.
\]

\section*{Pop Quiz Q2}

$A = \text{int8}[128, 2048]$, \quad $\text{mesh} = \{X{:}2,\, Y{:}8,\, Z{:}2\}$, \quad sharding: $A[I_{XY}, J]$.

\[
\text{local shape} = \text{int8}\!\left[\tfrac{128}{16},\, 2048\right] = \text{int8}[8, 2048].
\]

\begin{align*}
\text{per-device } A \text{ mem usage} &= 8 \times 2048 \text{ bytes} = 16{,}384 \text{ bytes}. \\
\text{across all devices} &= 2 \cdot 8 \cdot 2 \cdot 16{,}384 \text{ bytes} = 524{,}288 \text{ bytes}.
\end{align*}

\section*{Pop Quiz 2 [AllGather time]}

$\text{AllGather}_Y([E_Y, F]) \to [E, F]$ on TPU v5e. $\text{mesh} = \{X{:}8, Y{:}4\}$.

The 2D mesh maps onto the logical device mesh. TPU v5e pod is $16 \times 16$ so this slice has \underline{NO} wraparound links so we can't do a bi-directional AllGather. (Though note, in a mesh each axis doesn't have to map to a specific hardware axis.)

\subsection*{(i) $E = 2048,\; F = 8192,\;$ bf16}

local shape: $\text{bf16}\!\left[\tfrac{2048}{4}, 8192\right] = \text{bf16}[512, 8192]$.

\[
\Ttotal = \max\!\left[\Tmin \cdot (4-1),\; \frac{2 \cdot 512 \cdot 8192}{4.5 \times 10^{10}} \cdot (4-1)\right]
= \max[3\,\mu\text{s},\; 5.59 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{s}] = \max[3\,\mu\text{s},\; 559\,\mu\text{s}].
\]

This workload is throughput bound: $\Ttotal = 559\,\mu\text{s}$.

\subsection*{(ii) $E = 256,\; F = 256,\;$ bf16}

local shape: $\text{bf16}\!\left[\tfrac{256}{4}, 256\right] = \text{bf16}[64, 256]$.

\[
\Ttotal = \max\!\left[1\,\mu\text{s} \cdot (4-1),\; \frac{2 \cdot 64 \cdot 256}{4.5\mathrm{e}10} \cdot (4-1)\right]
= \max[3\,\mu\text{s},\; 2.18\,\mu\text{s}].
\]

This load is latency bound: $\Ttotal = 3\,\mu\text{s}$.

\section*{AllGather and ReduceScatter as derivatives of each other --- small example}

Let $n = 2$ (shard size), $p = 3$ (\# of devices), $u = [1, \ldots, 1]^T \in \mathbb{R}^p$.

Say on forward pass we do an AllGather:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item $x \in \mathbb{R}^{pn}$ (shard of size $n$ on $p$ devices).
  \item $\text{AllGather}(x) : \mathbb{R}^{pn} \to \mathbb{R}^{p^2 n}$ (all shards on all devices).
\end{itemize}

\begin{align*}
\text{AllGather} = u \otimes I_{pn} = \begin{bmatrix} I_{pn} \\ \vdots \\ I_{pn} \end{bmatrix}
&\in \mathbb{R}^{p^2 n \times pn} = \mathbb{R}^{3^2 \cdot 2 \,\times\, 3 \cdot 2} = \mathbb{R}^{18 \times 6} \\
&= u \otimes I_n \otimes I_p = \text{broadcast} \otimes I_p \\
&= \begin{bmatrix} I_6 \\ \vdots \\ I_6 \end{bmatrix} \quad p \text{ stacked identity blocks}.
\end{align*}

On backward pass: we have $\partial \ell / \partial y$; on forward we did $y = \text{AllGather}(x)$ (matrix).
\[
\Rightarrow \frac{\partial \ell}{\partial x} = (\text{AllGather})^T\, \frac{\partial \ell}{\partial y}.
\]
\begin{align*}
(\text{AllGather})^T = (\text{broadcast} \otimes I_p)^T &= \text{reduce} \otimes I_p \\
&= u^T \otimes I_n \otimes I_p \\
&= u^T \otimes I_{pn} \\
&= \text{ReduceScatter}.
\end{align*}
\[
\Rightarrow \frac{\partial \ell}{\partial x} = \text{ReduceScatter}\, \frac{\partial \ell}{\partial y}.
\]
\begin{align*}
\text{ReduceScatter} = u^T \otimes I_{pn} = [1\;\cdots\;1] \otimes I_{pn}
&= [I_{pn}\;\cdots\;I_{pn}] \\
&= \underbrace{[I_6\;\cdots\;I_6]}_{p \text{ identity blocks}} \in \mathbb{R}^{6 \times 18}.
\end{align*}

\section*{Q2}

TPU v4p $4 \times 4 \times 4$ slice $\Rightarrow$ full cube so we have wraparound links $\Rightarrow$ bidirectional ICI b/w.
$\text{mesh} = \{X{:}4, Y{:}4, Z{:}4\}$. Assuming ICI link overhead $\Tmin = 1\,\mu\text{s}$.

\subsection*{(i) $\text{AllGather}_x([B_x, D_y])$, $B = 1024$, $D = 4096$, bf16}

$B$ is only sharded along $X$ so $Y$ dim comm happens in parallel.

Each device sends a chunk of $\dfrac{2 \cdot 1024 \cdot 4096 \cdot 2}{4 \cdot \cancel{X}}$ per hop (bidirectional), over $\tfrac{4}{2} = 2$ hops.

\[
\Thop = \max\!\left(\Tmin,\; \frac{(1024 \cdot 4096 / 4)}{9 \times 10^{10}}\right) = \max(1\,\mu\text{s},\; 11.6\,\mu\text{s}) = 11.6\,\mu\text{s}.
\]
(this op is throughput bound)

\[
\Ttotal = \Thop \cdot \tfrac{4}{2} = 11.6\,\mu\text{s} \cdot 2 = 23.2\,\mu\text{s}.
\]

\subsection*{(ii) $\text{AllGather}_{xy}([B_x, D_y]) = [B, D]$, $B = 1024$, $D = 4096$, bf16}

Multiple axes to gather over.

\begin{align*}
\Ttotal &= \max\!\left[\frac{\Tmin \cdot 8}{2},\; \frac{2 \cdot B \cdot D}{\Wici \cdot 2}\right] \\
        &= \max\!\left[4\,\mu\text{s},\; \frac{2 \cdot 1024 \cdot 4096}{9 \times 10^{10} \cdot 2}\right] \\
        &= \max[4\,\mu\text{s},\; 46\,\mu\text{s}].
\end{align*}
Throughput bound: $\Ttotal = 46\,\mu\text{s}$.

\subsection*{(iii) $\text{AllReduce}_z([B_x, D_y]\{U_z\}) = [B_x, D_y]$}

Can be split up into:
\begin{align*}
\text{ReduceScatter}_{z,D}([B_x, D_y]\{U_z\}) &= [B_x, D_{yz}] \\
\text{AllGather}_z([B_x, D_{yz}]) &= [B_x, D_y]
\end{align*}

\[
\Ttotal = 2 \cdot \frac{BD/(xy)}{\Wici}
       = 2 \cdot \frac{1024 \cdot 4096 \cdot 2}{4 \cdot 4 \cdot 9 \times 10^{10}}
       = 11.6\,\mu\text{s}.
\]
Throughput bound.

\section*{Q4}

$X[B, D] \cdot Y[D_x, F] \to Z[B, F]$ \quad (assuming bf16 precision).

\subsection*{Strategy 1}

$\text{AllGather}_x(Y[D_x, F])$ first and then multiply.

\begin{align*}
\Tcomm &= \frac{2 \cdot D \cdot F}{\Wici} \quad \text{(bidirectional)} \\
\flops &= 2BDF \\
\Tcomp &= \frac{2BDF}{\text{accel }\flopss}
\end{align*}

\[
\text{Lower bound on } \Ttotal = \max\!\left(\frac{2DF}{\Wici},\; \frac{2BDF}{\text{accel }\flopss}\right).
\]

\subsection*{Strategy 2}

Do local sharded matmul $X[B, D_x] \cdot Y[D_x, F] \to Z[B, F]\{U_x\}$, then:
\begin{align*}
\text{AllReduce}_x(Z[B, F]\{U_x\}) &\to Z[B, F] \\
= \text{ReduceScatter}_{x, F}(Z[B, F]\{U_x\}) &\to Z[B, F_x] \\
\text{AllGather}_x(Z[B, F_x]) &\to Z[B, F].
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\Tcomm &= 2 \cdot \frac{2BF}{\Wici} = \frac{4BF}{\Wici} \\
\flops &= \frac{2BDF}{X},\quad \Tcomp = \frac{2BDF / X}{\text{accel }\flopss}
\end{align*}

\[
\text{Lower bound } \Ttotal = \max\!\left(\frac{4BF}{\Wici},\; \frac{2BDF}{X \cdot \text{accel }\flopss}\right).
\]

\subsection*{Comparing strategies}

If our matrix is very large such that the time in Strategy 1 is greater than twice the comm cost, Strategy 2 makes more sense.

To show this (assuming we're on a large model and throughput bound for our comms):

Strat 1 is comm bound when
\[
\frac{2DF}{\Wici} > \frac{2BDF}{\text{accel }\flopss}
\;\Rightarrow\;
\frac{\text{accel }\flopss}{\Wici} > B \quad \text{(in bf16)}.
\]

Using TPU v5p for values going forward:
\[
\Rightarrow B < \frac{4.59 \times 10^{14}}{1.8 \times 10^{11}} = 2550.
\]

Strat 2 is comm bound when
\[
\frac{2 \cdot 4BF}{\Wici} > \frac{2BDF}{X \cdot \text{accel }\flopss}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{2}{\Wici} > \frac{D}{X \cdot \text{accel }\flopss}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{D}{X} < \frac{2 \cdot \text{accel }\flopss}{\Wici} = 5100.
\]

Both are typically comms bound for LLM workloads, so comparing $\Tcomm$:
\[
\Tcomm^{\text{strat 1}} = \frac{2DF}{\Wici}, \qquad \Tcomm^{\text{strat 2}} = \frac{4BF}{\Wici}.
\]
\[
\Tcomm^{\text{strat 2}} < \Tcomm^{\text{strat 1}}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{4BF}{\Wici} < \frac{2DF}{\Wici}
\;\Rightarrow\; 2B < D \;\Rightarrow\; B < \frac{D}{2}.
\]

Assuming $D \approx 8000 \Rightarrow B < 4000$, which is typically true.

For large batch sizes, Strat 1 becomes comp bound while Strat 2 still stays comm bound as it depends on $X$ being very small, which is almost never true for autoregressive workloads. For Strat 2 to still be better:
\[
\Tcomm^{\text{strat 2}} \geq \Tcomp^{\text{strat 1}}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{4BF}{\Wici} < \frac{2BDF}{\text{accel }\flopss}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{2}{\Wici} < \frac{D}{\text{accel }\flopss}
\]
\[
\Rightarrow D > \frac{2 \cdot \text{accel }\flopss}{\Wici} = 5100.
\]

For LLMs, $D \approx 8\text{k}$, so this is almost always true.

Therefore, for LLM workloads, Strat 2 would almost always be better.

\section*{Q5}

(bf16)\quad $A[I, J] \cdot_J B[J, K] \to C[I, K]$ on TPU v4p $4 \times 4 \times 4$.

Want lowest latency, can start w/ any input sharding but result needs to be fully replicated.

Try different sharding computation cases.

\emph{Note:} \# of devices inputs are sharded along do \underline{NOT} affect $\Ttotal$ of the comm since the output shape is fixed (assuming we're in bw bound regime).

\subsection*{Case 1 (Not sharded along contr dim / not fully replicated)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item $A[I_{xyz}, J] \cdot B[J, K] \to C[I_{xyz}, K]$
  \item $\text{AllGather}_{xyz}(C[I_{xyz}, K]) \to C[I, K]$
\end{enumerate}

\[
\Tcomm = \frac{2 \cdot IK}{\Wici \cdot 3} = \frac{2IK}{3 \cdot 9 \times 10^{10}}.
\]

How are sharded doesn't really matter since the AllGather cost will stay about the same.

\[
\text{FLOPS per TPU} = 2\left(\frac{I}{xyz}\right) \cdot J \cdot K = \frac{2JKI}{xyz} = \frac{2JKI}{64} = \frac{JKI}{32}.
\]

Regardless of how we split up the sharding, FLOPS/TPU stay the same (unless we duplicate along some hardware axis, but this would be suboptimal as we'll just have more FLOPS/TPU to do).

\subsection*{Case 2 (Fully duplicated on each device)}

$A[I, J] \cdot B[J, K] \to C[I, K]$.

No comm cost, but very large matrices may not be able to fit on a single TPU.

\[
\Tcomp = \frac{2IJK}{\text{accel }\flopss} = \frac{2IJK}{2.75 \times 10^{14}}.
\]

\subsection*{Case 3 (one operand has a sharded contr dim)}

$A[I, J_{xyz}] \cdot B[J, K] \to C[I, K]$.

This case is suboptimal to Case 1 no matter what kind of sharding variation you try since you have to AllGather at the start and will have more FLOPS/TPU to do along with potentially having to do a final AllGather.

\subsection*{Case 4 (both operands are sharded along contr dim)}

$A[I, J_{xyz}] \cdot B[J_{xyz}, K] \to C[I, K]\{U_{xyz}\}$.
\[
\text{AllReduce}_{xyz}(C[I, K]\{U_{xyz}\}) \to C[I, K].
\]

Any other sharding pattern would be suboptimal since we'd have to do either more FLOPS/TPU or an extra AllGather at the end.

\[
\Tcomp = \frac{2IJK / (xyz)}{\text{accel }\flopss}, \qquad \Tcomm = \frac{2 \cdot 2IK}{\Wici \cdot 3}.
\]

\subsection*{Comparison}

\begin{align*}
\Ttotal^{\text{case 1}} &= \max\!\left\{\frac{2IK}{3 \cdot 9 \times 10^{10}},\; \frac{IJK/32}{2.75 \times 10^{14}}\right\} \\
\Ttotal^{\text{case 2}} &= \frac{2IJK}{2.75 \times 10^{14}} \\
\Ttotal^{\text{case 4}} &= \max\!\left\{\frac{4IK}{3 \cdot 9 \times 10^{10}},\; \frac{IJK/64}{2.75 \times 10^{14}}\right\}
\end{align*}

Case 4 has the same FLOPS/chip as Case 1 but double the comm cost. Case 2 has no comm cost but in most LLM cases may not even be feasible.

$\Rightarrow$ \textbf{Lowest latency sharding:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $A[I_{xyz}, J] \cdot B[J, K] \to C[I_{xyz}, K]$
  \item $\text{AllGather}_{xyz}(C[I_{xyz}, K]) \to C[I, K]$
\end{enumerate}
\[
\Ttotal = \max\!\left\{\frac{2IK}{3 \cdot 9 \times 10^{10}},\; \frac{IJK/32}{2.75 \times 10^{14}}\right\}.
\]

\section*{Q6}

$4 \times 4$ v5e slice has no wraparound connection $\Rightarrow$ can't use bidirectional ICI.

\subsection*{(i) $A[I_x, J_y] \cdot_J B[J_y, K] \to C[I_x, K]$ on TPU v5e $4 \times 4$}

\begin{align*}
A[I_x, J_y] \cdot_{\text{LOCAL}} B[J_y, K] &\to C[I_x, K]\{U_y\} \\
\text{AllReduce}_y(C[I_x, K]\{U_y\}) &\to C[I_x, K]
\end{align*}

\Tcomp = \frac{2 \cdot (I/X) \cdot (J/Y) \cdot K}{\text{v5e }\flopss}
       = \frac{2IJK}{XY \cdot \text{v5e }\flopss}
       = \frac{2IJK}{16 \cdot 1.97\mathrm{e}14} \quad \text{(assuming bf16)}.
\]
\[
\Tcomm = 2 \cdot \frac{(IK \cdot 2 / X) \cdot (Y - 1)}{Y \cdot \Wici} \quad \text{(unidirectional)}
       = \frac{4IK(Y-1)}{XY \Wici} = \frac{12 IK}{16 \cdot 4.5\mathrm{e}10}.
\]

\subsection*{(ii) $A[I_x, J] \cdot_J B[J_x, K_y] \to C[I_x, K_y]$}

\begin{align*}
\text{AllGather}_x(B[J_x, K_y]) &\to B[J, K_y] \\
A[I_x, J] \cdot_J B[J, K_y] &\to C[I_x, K_y]
\end{align*}

\[
\Tcomp = \frac{2 \cdot (I/X) \cdot J \cdot (K/Y)}{\text{v5e }\flopss}

\]

\section*{Q7}

Transformer block ($F \gg D$):
\[
\text{In}[B, D] \cdot W_{\text{in}}[D, F] \cdot W_{\text{out}}[F, D].
\]
$D = 8192$, $F = 32768$, $B = 128$, bf16, TPU v5e $2 \times 2$ (no wraparound / bidir ici).
Each TPU has only $300$ MB $= 3 \times 10^8$ bytes of free memory.

\begin{align*}
\text{sizeof}(\text{In}) &= 2 \cdot B \cdot D = 2 \cdot 128 \cdot 8192 = 2{,}097{,}152 \approx 2 \text{ MB} \\
\text{sizeof}(W_{\text{in}}) &= 2 \cdot D \cdot F = 2 \cdot 8192 \cdot 32768 \approx 536 \text{ MB} \\
\text{sizeof}(W_{\text{out}}) &= 2 \cdot F \cdot D \approx 536 \text{ MB}
\end{align*}

$\text{sizeof}(W_{\text{in}}[D, F_{xy}]),\; \text{sizeof}(W_{\text{out}}[F_{xy}, D]) = 131.5$ MB.

Intermediate output also needs to fit in TPU memory.

\subsection*{Try 1}

\begin{align*}
\text{In}[B, D] \cdot W_{\text{in}}[D, F_{xy}] \cdot W_{\text{out}}[F_{xy}, D]
&= \text{Tmp}[B, F_{xy}] \cdot W_{\text{out}}[F_{xy}, D] \\
&= \text{Out}[B, D]\{U_{xy}\}
\end{align*}
\[
\text{AllReduce}_{xy}(\text{Out}[B, D]\{U_{xy}\}) = \text{Out}[B, D].
\]

Memory used per TPU:
\begin{align*}
&\text{sizeof}(\text{In}[B, D]) + \text{sizeof}(W_{\text{in}}[D, F_{xy}]) + \text{sizeof}(W_{\text{out}}[F_{xy}, D]) \\
&\quad + \underbrace{\text{sizeof}(\text{Tmp}[B, F_{xy}])}_{2 \cdot 128 \cdot 32768/4 \,=\, 2 \text{ MB}}
+ \underbrace{\text{sizeof}(\text{Out}[B, D])}_{2 \cdot 128 \cdot 8192 \,=\, 2 \text{ MB}} \\
&= 2\text{ MB} + 131.5\text{ MB} + 131.5\text{ MB} + 2\text{ MB} + 2\text{ MB} = 269\text{ MB} \;\checkmark\; \text{fits}.
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\Tcomp &= \frac{(2 \cdot BD \cdot F/(XY)) + (2 \cdot B \cdot F/(XY) \cdot D)}{\text{v5e }\flopss}
       = \frac{2BDF}{XY \cdot \text{v5e }\flopss} \\
       &= \frac{2 \cdot 128 \cdot 8192 \cdot 32768}{4 \cdot 1.97\mathrm{e}14} = 87\,\mu\text{s} \\
       &\qquad \text{(time for each matmul is } 43.5\,\mu\text{s)}.
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\Tcomm &= 2 \cdot \frac{(2 \cdot BD) \cdot (X + Y - 1)}{XY \cdot 2 \cdot \underbrace{\Wici}_{\text{unidir}}}
       = \frac{2BD(X + Y - 1)}{XY \Wici} \\
       &= \frac{2 \cdot 128 \cdot 8192 \cdot (4-1)}{4 \cdot 4.5 \times 10^{10}} \approx 34.9\,\mu\text{s}.
\end{align*}

In this case, the operation is comp bounded so the AR can be masked by the 2nd matmul. Therefore, the time under this sharding scheme gives the same latency as sharding $\text{In}[B, D_{xy}]$ and $\text{Out}[B, D_{xy}]$ even though full duplication may initially seem suboptimal.

    \item dev 2 sends dev 1: $B_2$
    \item dev 3 sends dev 0: $A_3$
    \item dev 3 sends dev 1: $B_3$
    \item dev 3 sends dev 2: $C_3$
  \end{itemize}

  Generalizing: in current 1D topology, every link follows this pattern where all devices traverse that link $X$ times depending on how many devices come before it and the links end.

  \[
  \Rightarrow (1 + 2 + \cdots + (D-1)) \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2} = \frac{(D-1)D}{2} \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2} = \frac{N^2 (D-1)}{2D}
  = \frac{N^2}{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{D}\right) \approx \frac{N^2}{2} \;\text{as}\; D \gg N.
  \]

  \item
  \[
  \frac{\text{Data}_{\text{AllToAll}}}{\text{Data}_{\text{AG/RS}}} = \frac{1}{2}.
  \]
  In an AG/RS, each device from its receiving link receives $(D-1)$ full shards $= (D-1) \cdot D$ smaller shards $= \big((D-1) + (D-1) + \cdots + (D-1)\big)$ smaller shards.

  For an AllToAll, we're sending across each link $(1 + 2 + \cdots + (D-1))$ smaller shards.

  Expanding it out, we simply transfer less data across devices since the end/start state of an AG/RS is each device having a tensor whose size is equal to the full array size, whereas for AllToAll, the before and after device tensor size is the same.

  \item The time halves since the data needs to travel less hops / ici links.

  \item With bidirectional links, every device before a link only uses the link to reach $\lfloor D/2 \rfloor$ devices after the links end.

  $\Rightarrow$ Across each link we now send
  \begin{align*}
  \left(\frac{D}{2} + \left(\frac{D}{2} - 1\right) + \cdots + 1\right) \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2}
  &= \frac{(D/2)(D/2 + 1)}{2} \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2} \\
  &= \frac{D^2/4 + D/2}{2} \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2}
  = \left(\frac{D^2}{8} + \frac{D}{4}\right) \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2} \\
  &= \frac{D^2 + 2D}{8} \cdot \frac{N^2}{D^2}
  = \frac{1 + (2/D)}{8} \cdot N^2 \\
  &= \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{N^2}{2}\left(1 + \frac{2}{D}\right), \quad\text{as } D \gg N \\
  &\Rightarrow \frac{N^2}{8} \quad (\text{4 times lower time}).
  \end{align*}

  \item
  \[
  \frac{T^{\text{AG,bidir}}}{T^{\text{AllToAll,bidir}}} = \frac{N^2 / 2}{N^2 / 8} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 8 = 4.
  \]
  As mentioned in (3), the speedup happens b/c we're not sending the whole shard from one device to other devices each time. When we add bidirectional links to our AllToAll, we get to send less data across each link along with taking less hops. Our original AllToAll (unidir) was $2\times$ the unidir AG. Adding bidir links $2\times$ the speed of the AG but $4\times$ the AllToAll, hence the ratio of 4.
\end{enumerate}
